# Five-row boundary families: explicit definitions

Status: definitions derived from the handoff (§8–§13), the five-row induction
note, the White-first note, the DX/interface note and the round-four notes,
then **cross-checked against every recorded value** listed below. No mismatch
was found, so these definitions are the ones used for all computations in
this directory. Explicit masks for widths 1–12 of every family are in
[`families_masks.json`](families_masks.json), generated by
[`families.py`](families.py).

## Coordinates, masks, permission symbols

* Board: 5 rows `r = 0..4` (top to bottom) × `n` columns `c = 0..n−1`.
* Masks are row-major with bit `r·n + c`; bit 0 is the top-left cell.
  `A` = Blue-legal cells, `B` = White-legal cells.
* Diagram symbols are permissions, not stones: `o` both, `b` Blue only,
  `w` White only, `.` neither (a dead cell, deleted from the graph).
* Blue at v: `(A,B) → (A∖N[v], B∖{v})`; White at v: `(A,B) → (A∖{v}, B∖N[v])`.
* Blue = Left = positive; `G ≤ 0` iff Blue loses moving first.

## Endpoint letters (read top → bottom, rows 0..4)

| Letter | Pattern | Blue-forbidden row | White support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D | `obwbo` | 2 | {0,2,4} |
| U | `wbobo` | 0 | {0,2,4} |
| V | `bwbob` | 1 | {1,3} |
| X | `bowob` | 2 | {1,2,3} |
| R | `wobob` | 0 | {0,1,3} |
| J | `owobo` | 1 | {0,1,2,4} |
| T | `bobob` | none | {1,3} (White-first note only) |

Each of D,U,V,X,R,J has exactly one White-only cell, at its Blue-forbidden row.

## Two-ended families `PQ_n`

`PQ_n` is the 5×n strip whose column 0 carries letter P and whose column
n−1 carries letter Q, **both read top→bottom** (the right letter is not
vertically flipped); every other column is neutral (`ooooo`).

* **Width-one convention.** At `n = 1` the single column is the
  *intersection*: a cell is Blue-legal iff Blue-legal in both P and Q, and
  White-legal iff White-legal in both. Examples: `DU_1 = wbwbo`,
  `DJ_1 = o.wbo`, `DV_1 = b..bb`, `DD_1 = obwbo`.
* Horizontal reflection maps `PQ_n` to `QP_n` (same value). Hence
  `XR_k = RX_k` and `XV_k = VX_k` exactly; they are listed separately
  only because the DX obstruction names them in that orientation.
* D and X are vertically symmetric, so e.g. `DU` and `D(Ū)` (U flipped
  vertically) are vertical mirrors with equal values; the orientation of
  a single non-symmetric endpoint relative to another non-symmetric
  endpoint (e.g. `UU` vs `UŪ`) is **not** a symmetry and is not identified.

Families used: `DD, DU, DV, DR, DX, DJ` (D-ended), the paired targets
`RX, VX, XX`, and the X-pairs `XU, XR, XV, XJ` forced by the DX one-column
obstruction (`dx_boundary_obstruction.md`: a row-0 opening forces XU or XR,
a row-1 opening forces XV or XJ on the side retaining the X end). Also
`DT` (White-first note).

## One-ended families `P_n`

`P_n` carries letter P on column 0 and neutral columns elsewhere
("one restricted end and one neutral end", handoff §9; `D_n` is defined in
§8 with first column `obwbo`). `P_1` is the single column P.

```text

oooo   wooo   booo   booo   wooo   oooo
booo   booo   wooo   oooo   oooo   wooo
wooo   oooo   booo   wooo   booo   oooo
booo   booo   oooo   oooo   oooo   booo
oooo   oooo   booo   booo   booo   oooo
```

## Coupled bridge family `M_n` (odd n)

`M_n` is the actual empty 5×n position after Blue `(2,(n−1)/2)` and White
`(0,0)`; **Blue is next**. No cell is relaxed. Masks agree with the round-four
bridge certificates: `M_3 = (31278, 32628)`, `M_5 = (33408894, 33550300)`,
`M_7 = (34342501374, 34359607164)`, `M_9 = (35182209916926, 35184367894012)`.

```text
M_5      M_7        M_9
.booo    .booooo    .booooooo
bowoo    boowooo    booowoooo
ow.wo    oow.woo    ooow.wooo
oowoo    ooowooo    oooowoooo
ooooo    ooooooo    ooooooooo
```

## Paired-target notation

`T_q(G)` means `G ≤ q` **and** `G + * ≤ q` (round-four star-bound note); it
implies `G < q` and `G + * < q`.
